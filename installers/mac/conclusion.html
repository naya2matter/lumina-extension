One more step

Chrome needs you to load the extension once (a one-time Chrome requirement for privately-distributed extensions):

  1. Open Chrome and go to chrome://extensions
  2. Turn ON "Developer mode" (top-right) — leave it on.
  3. Click Load unpacked (top-left).
  4. Choose the extension folder that just opened in Finder (~/Library/Application Support/PNE LC AI/extension).

Updates apply automatically in the background from our server — you won't need to repeat this.

If after a Chrome update the extension disappears, reopen chrome://extensions, confirm "Developer mode" is ON, and toggle "PNE LC AI" back on. No reinstall needed.
