Privacy Policy — PNE LC AI (Gemini Connector)

Last updated: 13 July 2026

This privacy policy describes how the PNE LC AI — Gemini Connector Chrome extension ("the extension") handles user data. The extension is provided by PNE LC AI to let a user connect their own Google Gemini session to their own PNE LC AI account.

What the extension does

When the user clicks Connect Gemini Automatically, the extension reads the user's Google Gemini session cookies (__Secure-1PSID and __Secure-1PSIDTS) and the user's PNE LC AI session token, and sends the cookies directly to the user's own PNE LC AI backend so that PNE LC AI can access Gemini on the user's behalf.

Data we handle

  • Authentication information — Google Gemini session cookies and the user's PNE LC AI session token.

We do not collect personally identifiable information, health, financial, location, browsing history, personal communications, or website content.

How the data is used

  • Data is accessed only when the user explicitly initiates a connection from the extension popup.
  • Session cookies are held in memory only, transmitted directly to the user's own PNE LC AI backend (https://backend.ai.lcportal.cloud), and are never stored, logged, or retained by the extension.
  • The optional "Copy Cookies" button copies the cookies to the user's clipboard at the user's request, for manual entry into PNE LC AI.

Data sharing

The extension does not sell or transfer user data to third parties. Data is sent only to the user's own PNE LC AI backend to fulfill the extension's single purpose. It is never used to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes, and never used for any purpose unrelated to connecting the user's Gemini session to PNE LC AI.

Data retention and deletion

The extension itself retains no data. Any data received by the PNE LC AI backend is governed by the PNE LC AI service terms and is used solely to maintain the user's Gemini connection.
